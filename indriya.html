Transformation Storyboard

From traditional campus → Smart Campus

A preview of what changes when INDRIYA findings are implemented through UniRP. Every statistic is grounded in Marwadi University's own digitisation journey.

1Admissions
Before
Paper-first admissions
  • Manual forms and data entry
  • Long queues at counters
  • No applicant visibility
  • Slow conversion pipeline
→
After (with UniRP)
Online self-service admissions
  • Online applications with document upload
  • Real-time status tracking
  • Automated fee collection and receipts
  • 40–60% faster conversion
Impact: Faster admissions, better applicant experience, higher conversion.
2Administration & Approvals
Before
File movement & signatures
  • Paper approvals across desks
  • Dependency on specific staff
  • No audit trail
  • Frequent approval delays
→
After (with UniRP)
Digital workflows
  • Mobile-first approvals
  • Auto-escalation & SLAs
  • Transparent tracking
  • ~70% faster turnaround
Impact: Faster, transparent, error-free approvals across the institution.
3Student Experience
Before
Physical queues & calls
  • Visit office for every request
  • No visibility to parents
  • Communication delays
→
After (with UniRP)
Mobile app & self-service
  • Unified student & parent app
  • Instant updates & payments
  • Fewer support tickets
Impact: Happier students, fewer complaints, empowered parents.
4Data & Decision Making
Before
Excel-based MIS
  • Reports take days to compile
  • Stale data by the time it reaches leadership
  • Guess-based decisions
→
After (with UniRP)
Real-time dashboards
  • Live KPIs for VC & registrar
  • Predictive academic analytics
  • Faster, confident decisions
Impact: Data-driven leadership — decisions grounded in real numbers.
